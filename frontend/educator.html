Divergent / Evolving Evidence

⋮
Scientific summary
a. What's True

Regular Vitamin C supplementation may slightly reduce the duration and severity of cold symptoms in some people.

b. What's Overstated

Vitamin C does not guarantee prevention or act as a cure for the common cold, and personal anecdotes are not scientific proof.
