Methodology

See how MedicaLogic checks a claim, where its evidence comes from, and how we test it.

How It Works

Step 1

You paste a health claim or a social media caption, in English or Arabic.

Step 2

We check it against our team’s database of verified claims. If it’s new, we search PubMed (systematic reviews first) and MedlinePlus live.

Step 3

The AI reads only the papers we found and writes a verdict and a short summary. It cannot cite anything we didn’t retrieve.

Step 4

The Emotional Manipulation Meter scores the wording, and claims we can’t answer are saved for our research team to review.

Evidence Standards

Our answers are built on PubMed research, with systematic reviews and meta-analyses ranked first, and on MedlinePlus pages from the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Every paper we cite is checked against PubMed before it is shown; any citation the AI invents is removed automatically. When the evidence is thin or mixed, we say so instead of guessing. MedicaLogic is an educational tool, not a medical diagnosis.

Our Team

Developed for the 2026 NYAS Junior Academy Challenge, MedicaLogic helps educators and health advocates fight the spread of health misinformation, including misinformation spread by AI, with tools that verify claims quickly. Behind it is a team that brought together each member’s strengths.
