Methodology

Discover how our advanced AI operates through transparent processes, rigorous evidence-based standards, and robust validation.

How the AI Works

Step 1

User enters viral claim.

Step 2

System queries PubMed Systematic Reviews directly (Zero-Memory RAG).

Step 3

Returns verified verdict badge + evidence summary.

Evidence Standards

To ensure absolute scientific integrity, our methodology relies on PubMed systematic reviews, prioritizing peer-reviewed data over secondary media claims. By grounding our insights in high-level, rigorously vetted clinical research, we eliminate noise and sensationalized reporting in favor of verified, evidence-based facts you can trust.

Our Team
